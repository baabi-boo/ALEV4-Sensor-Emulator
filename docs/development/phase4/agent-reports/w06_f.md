## Primary source checked

Independently retrieved the official EMVA PDF on 2026-11-16 UTC:
[EMVA Standard 1288, Release 4.0 Linear, 16 June 2021](https://www.emva.org/wp-content/uploads/EMVA1288Linear_4.0Release.pdf).
Locations below use the document's printed page numbers.

- Section 2.4, p. 9, Eq. (13): Poisson photoelectron counts have variance equal to mean.
- Section 3.1, p. 12, Eqs. (29)–(30): the dark-current mean and variance contributions
  scale with exposure; the zero-exposure terms are separate. Thermal counts are Poisson.
- Section 3.2, p. 12: Release 4 no longer assumes a specific temperature-increase model.
- Section 4, p. 13, before 4.1: PRNU describes sensitivity variation, DSNU spatial
  dark-signal variation, and DCNU spatial dark-current variation; DCNU is outside coverage.
- Preface p. 2 and Section 5, p. 17: compliance requires reporting mandatory camera
  measurements; implementing these equations does not establish compliance.

`docs/PHASE4_SOURCES.md` correctly summarizes these points and separately labels
its project assumptions. Its original 2026-11-15 America/Halifax retrieval record
was retained; this independent UTC recheck does not invalidate that earlier record.
No source-register edit was warranted.
