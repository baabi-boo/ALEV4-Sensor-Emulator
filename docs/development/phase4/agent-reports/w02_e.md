## Preregistered statistical contract

The implementation follows the quantitative design in `w01_f.md` and records
every sample count, analytical target, observed statistic, and bound.
The maximum pixel fixture is 512 by 512, or 262144 pixels. All statistical
readouts are checked for absence of physical or ADC clipping; no samples are
discarded. The dark-only doubled-exposure fixture runs after the previous
full-size result is released.

The declared total false-failure allowance is 0.01 under the ideal independent
draw model. The non-Gaussian family receives 0.005 for at most 32 scalar
comparisons. Its fixed multiplier is `sqrt(32/0.005) = 80`. Poisson and
Poisson-plus-Gaussian means and unbiased variances use their exact second and
fourth central moments, followed by Chebyshev and a union bound. Covariance
uses the known mean and its exact product variance. This is deliberately
conservative and is not described as an exact Gaussian tail for Poisson data.

The Gaussian family receives 0.005 for at most 64 scalar comparisons, with
`t = log(4*64/0.005) = 10.843494811027599`. Gaussian means use the normal-tail
bound. Known-mean second moments use two-sided chi-square concentration.
Covariance uses polarization of standardized jointly Gaussian variables;
the bound remains valid for shared components because the two polarized
variances sum to four. The suite counts the actual comparisons recursively
and rejects an undeclared inventory change or an exceeded allocation.

There are exactly **22 non-Gaussian and 59 Gaussian comparisons**, giving an
actual union-bound allocation of **0.008046875**. Fixed PRNG seeds establish
repeatability, not mathematical independence or calibrated camera behavior.

### Wrong-model controls

The controls use the same frozen acceptance predicates, not thresholds fitted
to the observed samples. Independent wrong-charge observations are disjoint
pairs; their separate analytical control checks are included in the 22-member
non-Gaussian inventory.

- Independent path charge gave covariance `0.2928904856`, rejected against
  the correct shared-charge target 100 with tolerance `32.3524293910` for
  the smaller control sample. Its difference second moment was
  `212.9228584212`, rejected against the correct interval
  `[12.7635153138, 13.2386356480]`.
- Reusing the high pixel stream in the low path gave covariance
  `6.0263518635`, rejected by the independence tolerance `0.0776748028`.
  Its difference second moment was `1.0043919772`, also rejected.
- Wrong-axis spatial pairs gave row/column covariances `-0.1729785399` and
  `0.3453714841`, rejected against the same-group target 25 with tolerance
  `2.7049811148`. Separate exact broadcast checks reject exchanged axes and
  independent pixel values masquerading as one shared row/column draw.
- Reused path-specific spatial streams and a reused frame stream are rejected.
  The latter gave covariance `24.5813591053` against the independent-frame
  tolerance `5.6746959896`, and zero difference noise also fails.

## RNG coverage and discovered defect

The seed schedule is independently enumerated in the test. All 17 component
tags are checked at both seed endpoints and immediately around every accepted
wrap boundary: 28 distinct frame seeds, 476 direct seed calculations, and
56 single/dual exposure calls. Independent seeded oracles check 504 Gaussian
component arrays and 112 Poisson component arrays. The Poisson oracle uses
the previously established `samplePoisson` implementation; the new statistical
suite supplies independent distributional checks.

Absolute sequence frame indices 1, 2, `2^26-1`, and `2^26` are exercised at
both sequence seed endpoints, including 136 retained component-seed checks
and no seed collisions among those tested frame/tag pairs.

Additional retained coverage is:

- 11 accepted seed representations and 14 invalid seed values.
- 98 invalid-seed calls across the pure helper and enabled/disabled core,
  spatial, and sequence APIs; eight invalid component names also reject.
- 23 zero-component toggles with 323 unchanged-array comparisons, covering
  shared, single, high, and low fixed/row/column/frame settings and all three
  active path pixel-read settings.
- 14 photo/dark policy and zero-support cases with 187 unchanged-array
  comparisons, including zero exposure, zero incident input, zero PRNU,
  neutral PRNU, zero dark rate, and independent shot switches.
- RNG-free model creation/map extraction, inactive-path changes, and identity
  labels; 12 enabled/disabled public success paths; 10 early failures; and
  six failures after draws or after an earlier sequence frame succeeds.
- Eight whole-result repeat pairs, including changed caller uniform/normal
  state, both seed endpoints, spatial results, and sequences. Four changed
  frame seeds change stochastic components while preserving fixed maps.

The first strict RNG run discovered that
`structuredNoiseSeed(single(4294967295),'photo')` accepted the represented
value `4294967296`. A mixed single/double range comparison rounded the upper
limit upward before the check. The same validator pattern affected sequence
seeds; disabled spatial dispatch also bypassed the corrected core validator.
The coordinator fixed the Phase 4 production validators by converting valid
numeric scalar representations to double before their range/integer checks
and applying the pure validation before disabled wrapper dispatch. This agent
made no production edits. The final strict suite retains the regression
across every Phase 4 public wrapper in both modes. Legacy source was unchanged.

A separate temporary diagnostic omitted only that known failing seed case
while the coordinator prepared the fix, to expose any further failures. It
was explicitly labelled diagnostic and is not acceptance evidence. The final
command below reran both complete, strict suites without omissions.
