## Analytical bounds and comparison inventory

`lambda+3*v^2`; the unbiased variance has variance
`[m4-(n-3)/(n-1)*v^2]/n`. The implemented mean and variance bounds are correct.
For centered `X=A+H,Y=A+L`, independent residuals give
`Var(XY)=lambda+2*a^2+a*(h+l)+h*l`; the photo/dark product variance is `64*36`.

`200+3*213^2`; known means, rather than fitted sample means, are used correctly.
Gaussian means and second moments each fail with probability at most `2*exp(-t)`.

so its covariance tolerance `sqrt(vx*vy)*(2*sqrt(t/n)+2*t/n)` costs at most `4*exp(-t)`.
Counts reconcile: non-Gaussian `7+7+2+6=22`; Gaussian pixel/row/column/frame/temporal `7+17+17+14+4=59`.
