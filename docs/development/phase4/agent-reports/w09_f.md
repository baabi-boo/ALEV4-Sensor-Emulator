## Supported bounds and practical conclusions

- The [fixture implementation](../../../../matlab/+alev4/phase4Fixtures.m) defines **24 demo frames, normally 6×8 with a 4×5 crop**. Acceptance also runs statistical suites and inherited gates; dividing its total duration by the demo-frame count would not measure simulation throughput.
- The [100,000,000-value bound](../../../PHASE4_USAGE.md#supported-sizes-and-allocation-checks) inventories represented numeric/logical values, including repeated fields, maps and diagnostics; sparse inputs count represented dimensions. It is not a pixel allowance or byte budget, and runtime sharing does not reduce the semantic count.
- Bundles independently cap actual payloads at **800,000,000 bytes**; simulation preflight can reject a request before that wire limit. Neither count includes every possible runtime/container/allocator/transient cost, and neither establishes aggregate peak memory or FPS.
- Static deduction from [the enabled direct-exposure guard](../../../../matlab/+alev4/simulateStructuredExposure.m): the profile's fully active **4608×3164 = 14,579,712 pixels** require **933,101,568 reserved values at 64/pixel alone**, exceeding 100 million before spectral/model overhead. Enabled direct full Open Gate therefore exceeds the current guard; geometry support and small coordinate-preserving crops do not establish full-frame structured execution. No full-frame input was allocated.
- These observations establish a bounded, modest fixture/evidence footprint for the recorded candidate and substantial observed CI timeout/disk headroom. They do not establish full Open Gate throughput, a general peak-memory guarantee, real-time operation, or performance on other configurations. Performance characterization remains separate work.
