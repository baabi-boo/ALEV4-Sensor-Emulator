## Findings integrated

The register separates published generic theory from the project's assumed
maps, distribution parameters, component placement, temporal independence and
EI policy. Dark-current rate variation and fixed electronic offsets have
different names, units, and exposure behavior. The reference does not justify
an arbitrary temperature-doubling law or an ALEV4 parameter claim.

Each planned parameter family has units, lifetime/support, equation or
distribution, source identifier, and applicable conditions. The analytical
variance/covariance and frame-difference checks are explicitly derived from
the chosen model and restricted to their unclipped conditions. Future measured
records require dataset and capture evidence, not merely a source label.

## Handoff

Keep the register aligned with finalized API/manifest names. Preserve the
assumption classification in constructors, examples and artifacts. The source
review establishes a defensible terminology boundary, not numerical
acceptance, camera calibration or EMVA compliance.
