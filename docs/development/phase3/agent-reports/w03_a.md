1. Nonuniform-grid hand crop `[4 6;8 10]`, source integral 66, correct cropped integral 28.
2. Same-extent conservation independently computed for three spectral planes, including exact zero.
3. Noninteger scale ratio and both horizontal/vertical singleton outputs; explicit subpixel offsets.
4. Constant preservation, linearity, nonnegativity, bounded output, and spectral-plane isolation.
5. Twenty-four deterministic fixtures compared against an independent four-loop rectangle-intersection oracle, without production sparse overlap matrices.
6. Exact cell averages of a known continuous step, with source resolutions 4 through 256 and an analytical crop-average error bound of source-cell width divided by pixel width. Error decreases from about 0.1174 to exact zero in the tested arithmetic; each error remains within its bound.
7. Complete-footprint coverage, including single-ULP missing support on every side. Missing coverage must not be snapped or normalized away.
8. Canonical grid numeric representations and invalid semantics/edges/areas; large-origin pixel-width distortion rejection.
9. Data shape/finite/nonnegative checks, sparse one-bin normalization, integer input, nonsquare/singleton grids, and spectral count retention.
10. Exact independent charge arithmetic proving that spatial sampling does not apply pixel area, fill factor, exposure, QE, or bin width twice. Density averages `[3,7]` produce incident counts `[6,14]` and expected electrons `[1.2,7]` for the selected parameters; changing fill factor changes charge but not the spatial footprint.
11. Exact aligned cropping and full seeded single/dual readout identity through the spatial wrapper. Dual configuration retains the same shared-shot readout behavior.
12. Explicit unsupported subnormal weighted arithmetic and optional integral-diagnostic overflow handling.
13. Caller RNG and input-data/configuration preservation on successful and rejected sampling.

## Defect found and fixed by parent

The initial sampler silently returned zero for a positive constant `eps(0)` under two half-cell weights. A first parent fix detected total disappearance, but the expanded oracle showed constants `3*eps(0)` and `5*eps(0)` being changed to `4*eps(0)`, respectively 33% high and 20% low. This is substantial error even though the exact constant average is representable.

Parent changed the nonaligned weighted path to reject positive subnormal inputs or outputs with `ALEV4:SpatialNumericRange`, while exact identity/aligned copies preserve their data. The final regression checks multiples `[1,3,5,7,9]*eps(0)` and permits only exact preservation or that explicit range error. The design records the supported numerical boundary. Production fixes were not made by this assignment.
